# Manual in-world acceptance checklist

This checklist records player-visible evidence separately from deterministic build and GameTest evidence. An unchecked item is not implied by a successful client startup.

## Grounded survival world

- [ ] Generate a fresh world and naturally locate silver and ruby ore
- [ ] Mine and process silver; craft the longsword, poleaxe, and halberd
- [ ] Confirm weapon pacing, names, icons, and first-person presentation are usable
- [ ] Recruit one foot soldier and one archer through survival recipes
- [ ] Exercise Follow, Hold, and targeted Attack with the command baton
- [ ] Confirm foreign, out-of-range, and over-cap units remain unaffected
- [ ] Save, exit, reload, and confirm soldier ownership/order/position behavior
- [ ] Confirm the archer fires and the foot soldier closes for melee combat
- [ ] Place palisade, reinforced stone, and both arrow-slit orientations
- [ ] Fire through both arrow-slit orientations and inspect collision visually

## Arthurian world profile

- [ ] Enable the Arthurian profile before starting the test world
- [ ] Craft and wear all four surcoats; confirm distinct inventory and worn presentation
- [ ] Construct a tournament venue from the grounds deed
- [ ] Complete melee and archery activities; confirm cooldown, tokens, laurel, and renown
- [ ] Construct Camelot; confirm the round table and named Bedivere, Gawain, and Lancelot entities
- [ ] Attempt a second Camelot charter before and after save/reload; confirm rejection and stable coordinates
- [ ] Confirm Young Knight default assignment and configured Arthur/Merlin transition gates
