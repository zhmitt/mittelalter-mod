# Outcome Guard

The repository keeps one stable product or operational outcome on the critical
path. Supporting tooling is non-blocking unless a concrete causal chain connects
it to an acceptance criterion or a high-risk security, privacy, data-integrity,
payment, safety, or Production concern.

Use `workflow/scripts/outcome-reset.sh` after two failed approaches or before
admitting a second supporting layer. The reset applies conservative safe
defaults, seeks proportional alternative release evidence, and parks optional
tooling instead of creating another blocking change.
